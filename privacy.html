Who we are

This website is operated by ROL SUMMIT LTD ("ROL SUMMIT", "we", "us" or "our"), a company registered in the United Kingdom, whose address is 84 Wood Lane, London, W12 0BZ, United Kingdom.

We are the data controller for the personal information collected through this website. We are committed to handling your information lawfully, fairly and transparently in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Information we collect

When you place an order

Orders are completed on Stripe's secure checkout. During checkout we collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and, where required, billing address, together with details of the items you buy.

Payment information

Your card details are entered directly into Stripe and are never sent to or stored on our servers. Stripe shares limited payment information with us, such as the card type, the last four digits and the payment status.

When you contact us

If you use our contact form or email us, we collect your name, email address, any phone number you choose to give, and the content of your message.

Technical information

Our hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, for security and to keep the website running reliably.

How we use your information

  • To process, deliver and support your order, including returns and refunds.
  • To take payment and help prevent fraud.
  • To reply to your questions and provide customer service.
  • To send you service messages about your order, such as confirmations and delivery updates.
  • To keep accounting and tax records and meet our other legal obligations.
  • To keep our website secure and improve how it works.

We do not sell your personal information. We will only send you marketing emails if you have clearly agreed to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Our legal bases

  • Contract: to process and deliver your order and handle returns.
  • Legal obligation: to keep financial records and comply with consumer, tax and other laws.
  • Legitimate interests: to prevent fraud, keep our website secure, respond to enquiries and improve our service, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent: where we ask for it, for example for marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Who we share it with

We only share personal information where needed to run our business and fulfil your order:

  • Stripe, our payment provider, to process payments and help prevent fraud. Stripe acts as an independent controller for some of this data; see Stripe's privacy policy for details.
  • Netlify, our website hosting provider, which also processes contact form submissions on our behalf.
  • Delivery partners and couriers, who receive your name, delivery address and phone number so they can deliver your order.
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, and public authorities such as HMRC, where required by law.

International transfers

Some of our service providers, including Stripe and Netlify, may process personal information outside the UK, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards recognised under UK law, such as adequacy regulations or the International Data Transfer Agreement and Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Order and transaction records: six years from the end of the financial year in which the order was placed, to meet tax and accounting requirements.
  • Customer service correspondence: up to two years after our last contact with you.
  • Contact form enquiries that do not lead to an order: up to 12 months.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use your browser's local storage to remember the items in your basket. This information stays on your device and is needed for the basket to work.

Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. When you check out, Stripe may use cookies on its checkout pages that are necessary for payment security and fraud prevention.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, where we have no legal reason to keep it;
  • ask us to restrict or stop processing your information in certain circumstances;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@rolsummit.shop. We will respond within one month and there is normally no charge.

Keeping your information secure

Our website is served over an encrypted connection, payments are handled by Stripe, and we limit access to personal information to those who need it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take appropriate steps to protect your information.

Children

Our website and products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page, with the date it was last updated.
