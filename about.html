Distinctive design. Modern living.

ROL SUMMIT LTD brings together contemporary furniture and home accessories for people who want their interiors to feel personal, refined and quietly memorable.

Our story

Too many homes are furnished from the same handful of ranges. We set out to offer something more individual.

ROL SUMMIT was created as a place to find pieces with a little more personality: furniture and objects with interesting shapes, considered details and honest materials, which still make sense in everyday life.

From our base in London we curate a focused collection of small furniture, storage, decorative accessories and statement pieces, and deliver them to customers across the UK. We keep the range deliberately edited, so every product earns its place.

How we choose each piece

Before anything joins the collection, it has to answer four questions well.

Form
Does it have a shape, proportion or detail that sets it apart from the ordinary?
Material
Are the materials and finishes chosen to look good, feel good and wear well over time?
Function
Does it solve a real need, whether storage, seating or surface, rather than simply fill space?
Longevity
Will it still feel right in five years, long after a passing trend has moved on?

Who we curate for

Design-conscious customers across the UK who value contemporary aesthetics, quality materials and individuality.

Whether you are furnishing a first flat, refreshing a single room or looking for a gift with real presence, our collection is for people who notice details and want their home to reflect them.
