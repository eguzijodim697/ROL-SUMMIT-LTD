An alternative to the ordinary

We look for the pieces most collections pass over: the side table with an unexpected silhouette, the storage that doubles as sculpture, the object you keep noticing.

ROL SUMMIT LTD is a London-based online retailer of contemporary furniture and home accessories. Every item is selected for its form, its materials and the way it lives day to day, so it works as well as it looks.

The result is a focused collection for people who want their home to feel considered and individual, without chasing trends.
