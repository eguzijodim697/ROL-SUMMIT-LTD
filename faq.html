Orders and payment

How do I place an order?

Add the pieces you would like to your basket, then select Checkout securely. You will be taken to Stripe's secure checkout page to enter your delivery and payment details. Once payment is complete, you return to our website and receive an email confirmation.

Can I order several items at once?

Yes. Add as many pieces as you like to your basket and pay for everything together in a single checkout.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept major debit and credit cards through Stripe. Depending on your device and browser, you may also see digital wallets such as Apple Pay or Google Pay at checkout.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment provider. Your card details are entered on Stripe's encrypted checkout page and never reach our servers.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Contact us as soon as possible at info@rolsummit.shop. If your order has not been dispatched, we will do our best to amend or cancel it. Once dispatched, you can still cancel under our refund policy.

Do you offer discount codes?

From time to time we run promotions. If you have a valid code, you can enter it on the checkout page before you pay.

Delivery

Where do you deliver?

We currently deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom.

How long will delivery take?

Most orders are dispatched within 3–5 working days and arrive 2–5 working days after dispatch. Larger furniture may need a scheduled delivery; if so, we will contact you to arrange a suitable date.

How much does delivery cost?

Any delivery charge for your order is shown clearly at checkout before you pay.

Can I track my order?

We keep you updated by email, including tracking details whenever the courier provides them.

What if I'm not at home for delivery?

Couriers usually leave a card or notification with options to rearrange delivery or collect your parcel. For scheduled deliveries of larger pieces, we agree the date with you in advance.

Returns and refunds

Can I return an item if I change my mind?

Yes. You can cancel your order within 14 days of delivery, then return the item within a further 14 days. Items should be unused, in their original condition and packaging. See our refund policy for full details.

What if my item arrives damaged or faulty?

Please contact us as soon as possible, ideally within 48 hours of delivery, with your order details and photos of the item and packaging. We will arrange a replacement, repair or refund. This does not affect your statutory rights.

When will I receive my refund?

We process refunds within 14 days of receiving your return, or of receiving proof that you have sent it back if that is sooner. Refunds go to your original payment method and can take 5–10 working days to appear, depending on your bank.

Who pays for return delivery?

If you are returning an item because you changed your mind, you pay the cost of returning it. If an item is faulty, damaged or not as described, we cover the return cost.

Products and care

Will my item look exactly like the photographs?

We photograph and describe every piece as accurately as possible. Natural materials such as wood and stone vary in grain, tone and texture, and screen settings can affect how colours appear, so small differences are normal.

Do pieces need assembly?

Many pieces arrive ready to use. Where assembly is needed, this is stated in the product details and instructions are included.

How should I care for my furniture?

Dust with a soft, dry cloth and avoid harsh cleaning products. Keep pieces out of prolonged direct sunlight and away from radiators, and use coasters or mats under hot or wet items. Any specific care notes are included with your order.

Will sold-out items come back?

Some pieces are available in limited quantities. If something you love is unavailable, contact us and we will let you know whether it is returning.
